# Security and Safety Policy

The SIGADAP project prioritizes human safety, privacy, and well-being
above all other considerations.

This document outlines the security posture and boundaries
of the project.

---

## Personal Safety

SIGADAP must not be used in any way that endangers
the physical, psychological, or social safety of any person.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- attempts to identify or locate individuals connected to the project;
- pressure, coercion, intimidation, or blackmail;
- forced disclosure of personal information;
- offline contact requests or attempts to establish real-world access.

Any such behavior is considered a violation of project boundaries.

---

## No Contact Policy

The project does not provide:

- personal contact information;
- real-time interaction channels;
- offline or in-person meetings;
- private discussions regarding the creator.

SIGADAP operates in an asynchronous, non-interactive mode by design.

---

## Threat Handling

If signs of misuse, pressure, or threat emerge:

- interaction may be discontinued immediately;
- no further explanation is required;
- silence is a valid safety response.

The project is not obligated to engage with
potentially unsafe interactions.

---

## Scope of Security

This repository contains only abstract concepts,
examples, and non-operational materials.

It does **not**:

- provide instructions for real-world influence;
- grant access to systems, infrastructure, or individuals;
- authorize any form of enforcement or authority.

---

## Final Note

Any engagement with SIGADAP assumes acceptance
of these security and safety boundaries.

Human life and dignity are non-negotiable.
