# CWDF Policy
Creator’s World Defence Framework

This document defines protective boundaries for the SIGADAP project.

The purpose of this policy is to protect the creator as a human being
and to ensure that the project cannot be used as a vector
for personal extraction, pressure, or control.

---

## 1. No Personal Reconstruction

The SIGADAP project must not be used to:

- infer, reconstruct, simulate, or speculate about the identity,
  biography, beliefs, psychological condition, or private life
  of its creator;
- present any part of the project as a direct reflection
  of a real individual;
- claim personal access to the creator through analysis of this work.

Any such attempts are explicitly prohibited.

---

## 2. No Targeting or Manipulation

The project must not be used to:

- target real individuals or groups;
- design psychological, ideological, or behavioral influence;
- justify pressure, coercion, blackmail, or intimidation.

SIGADAP is an observational and conceptual framework only.

---

## 3. No Obligations of Presence

The creator of SIGADAP:

- is not obligated to explain, defend, or elaborate on the project;
- may remain silent indefinitely;
- may stop, pause, or abandon the project without explanation.

No authority, spiritual or otherwise, is granted over the creator.

---

## 4. Interpretation Boundary

All interpretations must remain:

- conceptual, abstract, and structural;
- detached from real persons;
- free from biographical or psychological claims.

This project is not a window into a human life.

---

## 5. Safety First

Any use of SIGADAP that compromises human safety,
privacy, or physical well-being
is considered a violation of this framework.

Human life and safety have absolute priority
over any theoretical interpretation of this project.

---

## Summary

SIGADAP may be observed.

SIGADAP may be studied.

SIGADAP must not be used to extract, expose,
or endanger its creator or any other person.
